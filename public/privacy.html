Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

InvoiceGen ("we", "us", or "our") operates the InvoiceGen Shopify app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

Data We Access

InvoiceGen accesses the following data from your Shopify store solely to generate PDF invoices:

  • Order information (order number, line items, prices, currency)
  • Billing address (name, address) — used only for invoice rendering

Data Storage

InvoiceGen does not store, retain, or log any order data or customer information. PDFs are generated in real-time on our servers and immediately returned to you. No data is persisted beyond the duration of a single request.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to any third parties. Order data is used exclusively to render the PDF invoice and is discarded immediately after generation.

Authentication

We use Shopify OAuth 2.0 to authenticate your store. Your Shopify access token is stored in a signed, encrypted session cookie that expires after 24 hours. We do not store access tokens on our servers.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie (ig_session) to maintain your authenticated session. This cookie contains only your store domain and a temporary access token, signed with HMAC-SHA256.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info.shopworld.team@gmail.com.
